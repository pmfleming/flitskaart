A seventh tab that answers three questions at a glance: did I practise today, am I keeping it up, and is my Dutch getting stronger. It takes the habit view from Duolingo and Babbel and fills it with what this app already measures on every card.

What comes from Duolingo and Babbel

Both apps build their progress pages around the habit first and the learning second. This app has no server, no other learners and a calm look, so it keeps the habit view and drops the game around it.

PatternWhere it comes fromHere
Streak with a flameBoth. Babbel counts any completed activity towards it.Yes. Days in a row with a scored card, and the longest run so a broken streak isn’t a total loss.
The week at a glanceBabbel’s My Activity, Duolingo’s week rowYes. The last seven days, today on the right.
A goal for todayDuolingo’s daily goal, Babbel’s “Your goal”Yes, without a new setting. The goal is to empty today’s queue, the same finish line as Alles gedaan.
TotalsDuolingo’s total XP; Babbel’s lessons completed and time learnedYes, in real units. Cards learned and turns taken. Minutes come in step 3.
SkillsDuolingo’s year in review breaks practice down by kindYes. Speaking, writing and listening are already graded on every review.
How well words are knownReview features in bothYes. Four levels read from FSRS, which is more precise than either app shows.
XP, leagues, friendsDuolingoNo. Nobody to compare with, and XP would be an invented currency next to honest counts.
Streak freezeBoth; Babbel gives two a weekNot at first. See the decisions below.
Badges and milestonesDuolingoLater, if wanted. A quiet line at 7, 30 and 100 days would be enough.

Sources: Duolingo 101, Duolingo on leagues, Duolingo on badges, Babbel on building a habit, Babbel’s app interface.

What each number means

Every figure is worked out from the reviews and cards tables as they are today. A day is a local calendar day, the same boundary the daily limit for new cards already uses.

FigureRule
Practised dayAt least one saved review. Overslaan saves nothing, so it doesn’t count.
ReeksThe run of practised days ending today. Before today’s first card it shows the run ending yesterday with the flame in outline, because the streak is still alive until midnight.
VandaagCards scored today that aren’t due again right now, out of those plus what Store::counts says is waiting. A full ring is Alles gedaan.
Kaarten geleerdActive cards studied at least once.
Nieuw, Wankel, Groeit, SterkNever studied; then by FSRS stability, the number of days until the chance of recall falls to 90%: under 7, 7 to 30, and 30 or more. Paused cards are left out and counted beside the bar.
OnthoudenOf the reviews in the last 30 days that came at least a day after the card’s previous one, the share not scored Weet ik niet. Learning steps minutes apart are left out. The learner’s own score is used, since an unclear pronunciation is not forgetting. Under 10 such reviews it shows a dash.
Spreken, SchrijvenThe average speech_accuracy and written_accuracy over the last 30 days, with how often, and the change against the 30 days before.
LuisterenReviews done in Luisteren and the share known.
GetestThe share of reviews backed by speaking or writing. These are the reviews the scheduler trusts in full, so the number rewards the better way to practise.
ActiviteitReviews per day for 30 days, split into a card’s first review ever and the rest.

What the deck doesn’t record yet

  • Deleting a card deletes its reviews. Store::delete removes them, so deleting a card would erase practised days and could break a streak after the fact. The reviews should stay: nothing else reads them, and the strength figures look at cards only.
  • “Vandaag geoefend” forgets on restart. study.reviewed_today lives in memory, so Oefenen’s header and the rest screen start again at zero when the app reopens. They should read the same count as Voortgang.
  • Time isn’t measured. Both apps show minutes learned. A review only has the moment it was saved.
  • A conversation leaves no trace unless cards are made from it. Gesprek can’t count towards the streak or show up as a skill until it does.

Build order

  1. The numbers, in the core crate. A new crates/core/src/stats.rs shaped like usage.rs: a pure summarize(reviews, cards, now) that returns one Progress value, and Store::progress(now) that loads the rows for it. Tests feed it fixed rows: a run broken by a missed day, a day that crosses local midnight, learning steps left out of Onthouden, a card on each side of the 7 and 30 day limits. The limits are named constants beside the rule, as SCORE_ONLY_SHARE is. Store::delete stops removing reviews.
  2. The screen. Screen::Progress joins Screen::ALL as “Voortgang”, before Instellingen, so Ctrl+6 opens it and --screen voortgang works for screenshots. A new crates/app/src/progress.rs draws it from a Progress loaded in show(), the way Kaarten reloads there, and not on every frame. The day chart, day_label and the legend dot move from llm_settings.rs to ui.rs so Verbruik and Voortgang share them. Two icons join the set: rising bars for the tab and a flame. An empty deck gets the usual empty state pointing to Gesprek and Toevoegen.
  3. The streak where it is earned. The Alles gedaan card gains the flame and “12 dagen” among its stats, and Oefenen’s header and that card read today’s count from the store. The window test practises a card, opens Voortgang and checks a streak of one. README gets a Voortgang section.
  4. Time and Gesprek. A seconds column on reviews, from the card appearing to its score and capped at two minutes, gives “minuten geoefend” from that day on. A small talks table with one row per conversation (start, topic, the learner’s lines, how many were spoken) lets a conversation count as a practised day and adds a Gesprek row to Vaardigheden.
  5. Later, if the page earns it. A week ahead of cards falling due, the cards missed most often with a way to open them in Kaarten, and milestones.

Steps 1 to 3 need no change to the deck file. Step 4 adds a column and a table through the existing ADDED_COLUMNS path, so older decks open as before.

Decisions to confirm

  • Name and place. “Voortgang” as the sixth tab. Instellingen moves from Ctrl+6 to Ctrl+7. Seven tabs need a check at the 560-pixel minimum width.
  • What keeps a streak. One scored card. No freeze: the longest run stays on show, and a rest day that silently doesn’t count is harder to explain than a streak that restarts.
  • The strength limits. 7 and 30 days. They decide how soon “Sterk” starts to fill, and can move without touching saved data.
  • Whether step 4 is wanted. It is the only part that changes what the deck stores.
